# Looking for contributors

The goals below are looking for a contributor to do the implementation work. The goal summaries include details of the expected time commitment and work duration. If you might be interested in helping with a goal, please join the rust-lang Zulip and post a message in the [`#goals/proposed`][channel] channel: tell us a bit about yourself, and mention us (the goals team) in that message, like `Hello @T-goals!`. You could also write to the goals team at `goals-team@rust-lang.org`! We would love to help you learn more about it.
